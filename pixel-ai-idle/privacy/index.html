PIXEL AI IDLE

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Pixel AI Idle ("the game") is made by Oktay Dak, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the game uses and why. In short: the game has no accounts, I do not collect your personal data, your progress stays on your device, Apple handles purchases, Google AdMob shows ads, and the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics so I can improve it.

1. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (for example Compute, upgrades and achievements) and your settings (sound, music, voice, haptics and language) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to me or to any server I run. Deleting the game deletes this data. You can also erase it at any time in Settings → Reset Progress.

2. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see or store your payment details, name or Apple Account. The game only learns from Apple which pack you own so it can unlock it, and it can ask Apple to restore your purchases.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads, shown only when you tap a button to get a reward, and occasional short ad breaks at natural pauses (for example when switching tabs), at most once every few minutes. Any in-app purchase removes the ad breaks. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • device and advertising identifiers (Apple's IDFA only if you allow tracking),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device information (model, operating system, language),
  • ad interactions (for example which ads were shown or completed).

Learn how Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Anonymous gameplay statistics

From version 1.0.1 the game sends anonymous gameplay events to PostHog (servers in the EU) so I can see where the game is too hard, too slow or broken. Examples: the game was opened, a skill was learned, the lab grew, a purchase or ad finished or was cancelled, a setting was changed, an error occurred, and counts such as taps per session.

  • Each install gets a random identifier created by the game. It is not your Apple Account, IDFA or any other device identifier, and it is deleted when you delete the game.
  • No name, email, contacts, precise location or payment details are sent. Your IP address is not stored and is not used to locate you; the country comes from your device's language setting.
  • These statistics are not linked to your identity, not combined with other data and not used for advertising or tracking.

5. Your choices

  • Tracking (iOS): the game asks for permission before any tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalized using your IDFA. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): you are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can review or change your choice any time in the game's Settings → Privacy & ads.
  • Fewer ads: any purchase removes the ad breaks, and the Supporter Pack also gives rewards without ads.

6. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your rights

The anonymous statistics cannot be linked back to you; deleting the game removes its random identifier. You can also contact me to have the statistics from your install deleted. Data processed by Apple or Google is covered by their policies; you can use their tools to exercise your rights (for example under GDPR or CCPA). For any question, contact me and I will help.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
