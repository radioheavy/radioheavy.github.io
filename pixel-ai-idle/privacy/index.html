PIXEL AI IDLE

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Pixel AI Idle ("the game") is made by Oktay Dak, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the game uses and why. In short: the game has no accounts, I do not collect your personal data, your progress stays on your device, Apple handles purchases, and Google AdMob shows optional rewarded ads.

1. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (for example Compute, upgrades and achievements) and your settings (sound, music, voice, haptics and language) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to me or to any server I run. Deleting the game deletes this data. You can also erase it at any time in Settings → Reset Progress.

2. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see or store your payment details, name or Apple Account. The game only learns from Apple which pack you own so it can unlock it, and it can ask Apple to restore your purchases.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads: an ad is shown only when you tap a button to get a reward. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • device and advertising identifiers (Apple's IDFA only if you allow tracking),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device information (model, operating system, language),
  • ad interactions (for example which ads were shown or completed).

Learn how Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your choices

  • Tracking (iOS): the game asks for permission before any tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalized using your IDFA. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): you are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can review or change your choice any time in the game's Settings → Privacy & ads.
  • No ads at all: ads only appear when you request a reward. The Supporter Pack gives those rewards without ads.

5. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your rights

Because I do not collect personal data, I hold nothing to access, correct or delete. Data processed by Apple or Google is covered by their policies; you can use their tools to exercise your rights (for example under GDPR or CCPA). For any question, contact me and I will help.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
