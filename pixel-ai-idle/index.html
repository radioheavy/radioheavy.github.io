FAQ

I bought a pack but don't see it.
Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the game deletes your progress. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account and can always be restored.

How do I start over?
Settings → Reset Progress. Purchases are kept.

Do I have to watch ads?
No. Ads are optional and only appear when you choose a reward. The Supporter Pack gives those rewards without ads.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
Settings → Privacy & ads, or iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
